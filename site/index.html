Ten years of
getting it right

Aussie brunch culture, run by a Frenchman, on a Berlin side street. Café by day. Wine bar by night. Terrace all summer.

Allan came to Berlin by way of Australia and brought the good bits with him: the coffee, the eggs, the not-taking-it-too-seriously. Then he added the French half: rosé at lunchtime, oysters at six, a bar that stays open because the sun is still out.

It's been that way on Rykestraße for a decade. Some things haven't changed: the eggs Benny, the yellow awning, the fact that we still only take cash.

The brunch

The eggs Benny is mentioned in a hundred separate Google reviews. We're not going to pretend that's an accident.

  • The famous one

    Eggs Benny

    Two poached eggs, brioche, Allan's hollandaise. The single most-talked-about plate we do.

  • Add-on

    Benny + Allan's Lachs

    The Benny, with cured salmon on top. Pulled pork and truffled spinach are the other two ways to go.

  • Sweet

    French toast

    White chocolate, Greek yoghurt, oat streusel and thyme. There is an ABC Pavlova too.

  • Weekdays

    Open Grilled Cheese

    Cheddar, Bergkäse, caramelised onions, sriracha mayo and chimichurri, open on sourdough.

  • The French half

    Croque Monsieur

    Ham, Gruyère, caramelised onions, mushroom béchamel, brioche, chips. Add two poached eggs to make it a Madame.

  • Sweet

    Frenchamisu

    Brioche layered with banana and white chocolate mascarpone, finished with a shot of Baileys.

Our own label

Rosé all day,
everyday

There is a neon sign behind the bar that says exactly this, so we're committed now. New vintage lands every year and it does not hang around.

Beyond the ABC rosé there's a proper wine list, a frosé when it's hot, and a Bloody Mary that has rescued a number of Sundays.

Coffee, tea & the good stuff

Coffee roasted in Berlin. Bulletproof, dirty chai, espresso tonic, and a hot chocolate you can have with dark rum. Cocktails from the board.
